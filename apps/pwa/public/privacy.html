Privacy notice

What we collect

Mobile number for sign-in; family membership details and references; candidate biodata entered by the family and confirmed by the candidate; optional photos and supporting documents for verification. We do not collect Aadhaar numbers or home addresses.

Who can see it

Verified member families see only the profile code, age, education, profession and general location of candidates who have consented. Contact details are shared only when a candidate grants them to a specific person within an introduction. Verification documents are visible only to the assigned verifier and are deleted after review.

Your choices

Candidates can pause, withdraw, change visibility, revoke family permissions and stop sharing contact details at any time. Details already shared cannot be recalled. You can request a copy or deletion of your data from Settings.
